The goal of Don't Join was to tell a story from the perspective of Droids being forced to follow their programming in the Star Wars universe, to explore the horror of being trapped in a cycle of being forced to work with no escape.

Given the time limitations and my own lack of experience in writing, both in general and in writing an interactive narrative, many ideas that I had were cut down in an attempt to give a realistic scope that I was able to achieve in time for the due date.
